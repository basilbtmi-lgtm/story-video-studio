Privacy Policy

Story Video Studio · Last updated: November 6, 2026

Story Video Studio ("the app") is a personal application that creates short videos and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): the app requests the youtube.upload permission, which only allows it to upload videos to the signed-in channel. It does not read your videos, comments, subscribers, viewing history, email, contacts or any other Google account data.
  • Instagram (Meta): the app uses the Instagram Graph API to publish Reels to the connected Instagram professional account and to read that account's username and the link to each published post.

How the data is used

Access tokens are used solely to upload and publish videos that the account owner has created and approved in the app. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in local files that are not uploaded anywhere. The app has no servers and no database. No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties, other than sending the video and its caption to YouTube or Instagram when the owner chooses to publish.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for Instagram/Facebook under Settings → Business integrations / Apps and websites. Deleting the app's local credential files also removes the stored tokens.
