Story Video Studio

A personal tool for creating and publishing short story videos.

Story Video Studio is a private application used by its owner to create short vertical videos (startup stories, motivational quotes, motivational stories and true-crime stories) and to upload them to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

The app is not offered to the public. It only signs in to accounts that belong to its owner, and only uploads videos that the owner has reviewed and approved.
